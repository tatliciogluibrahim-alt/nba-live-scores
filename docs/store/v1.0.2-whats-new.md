# v1.0.2 — What's New (App Store)

A new editorial design, built for the knockout rounds.

- A calmer, sharper look across every screen. Big scores, clean rules,
  no cards, no clutter.
- Color that means something: vermilion for the chrome, green only
  when a match is live.
- Track a match on your lock screen in one tap. Up to three at once.
- Starting XI on every match page, with substitutions as they happen.
- Extra time and penalty shootouts now read correctly, minute by
  minute.
- Kickoff times always show the day when a match isn't today.
- The morning Brief now matches the app, with correct local times.
- Widgets and the Live Activity redesigned to match.

Follow what matters. Skip the rest.
