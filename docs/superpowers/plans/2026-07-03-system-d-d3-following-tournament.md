## Global Constraints

- Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-02-system-d-editorial-redesign-design.md` — §§1-6, 9, 10, 13 bind D3.
- **Register law (§1/§6):** Following gets exactly ONE ink band (the cross-link band). Tournament's one ink deployment is YOUR PATH. Everything else is cream agate. No rung-3 accent fields anywhere in D3 (nothing here is live-peak).
- **Tier stamps (§4 fill ladder):** `OFF` = Stamp variant `faint`, `QUIET` = `outline`, `COMPANION` = `filled`, `FULL` = `filledHeavy`. Stamp text `FULL` abbreviates the locked label "Full Details"; the legend always spells it out. Internal keys (`quiet|companion|all`) unchanged.
- **Tier legend (§4, exact copy):** "QUIET: start and final. COMPANION: key moments. FULL DETAILS: everything." One-time row under the first tier-stamped section head, dismissible (×, localStorage `no-noise-tier-legend-seen`), permanently reachable via a "?" affordance on that section head.
- **Accent law (§5 v2):** NO "advancing" dots in group tables (the mock's green dots beside qualified teams are a recorded mock defect) — the dashed cut line alone tells the story. The pulsing dot means live, exclusively.
- **Chrome law (§2):** Following is an app tab → Masthead (D1 component) on mobile. Tournament routes are leaf screens → crumb bar (`DetailCrumbs`, D2 component: left "← FOLLOWING", right "TOURNAMENT" / "GROUPS" / "BRACKET"). The mock's masthead-on-tournament is chrome the mocks omitted — spec overrides mock. TabBar persists on Following (an app tab); tournament leaf routes keep whatever chrome they have today outside the recomposed column.
- **Copy laws (§13):** "COMING UP" → "UP NEXT" (13.4). Follows count meta adaptive and never lying (13.5): "6 FOLLOWS · 3 OF 3 ALERT SLOTS USED". Alert-slots why-line (13.6): "Alerts on your first 3 follows are free." "SYNC" → "SYNC DEVICES" (13.10). "ALL 16 TIES" → "ALL 16 MATCHES" (13.1). En-dash in scores, no em-dashes user-facing. Sport nouns: MATCH/MATCHES for soccer, GAME/GAMES for NBA/NFL (13/§10). "Summer Soccer" stays the in-app tournament name.
- **Cut-line data integrity (controller amendment to §13.11, binding):** WC 2026 has 48 teams / 12 groups; top 2 per group qualify AND the 8 best third-placed teams. The mock footnote "TOP 2 GO THROUGH" is a confident lie by omission. Ship: cut line (dashed rule) after 2nd place, footnote "– – QUALIFICATION LINE · TOP 2 GO THROUGH · 8 BEST 3RDS JOIN". Never mark third place as eliminated.
- **No-Spoilers (§9):** all scores in knockout rows, group schedules, and any result-bearing line stay `Spoiler`-wrapped exactly as today; winner emphasis only where a winner exists (draws: both codes equal ink, `FT` stamp — §10).
- **Feature preservation (Do not silently remove):** FollowCard drawer (bell, tier picker, per-follow No-Spoilers, unfollow), FirstFollowTierCard, overlap hint, wrapped-slot nudge, Share + Sync modals, add-follow flow and pickers, TournamentPresetSection, AlertStatePill, SeasonWrappedBanner, NBA MiniSeriesStrip + momentum lines, WCGroups expandable schedules, WCBracket desktop columns, JSON-LD on tournament pages. All stay mounted (restyled on mobile, or untouched on desktop).
- Desktop md+ pixel-preserved until D4: every recomposition lives in a new `md:hidden` block; the existing layout moves verbatim into `hidden md:block`.
- Affordance law (§2): tappable rows get right-aligned mono `→` + pressed state; section heads not tappable (the "?" on the legend head is a distinct small control, not the head itself).
- Gates per task: `npm run lint` 0 warnings · `npm run build` (page count must not drop; no new routes expected) · `npm run test` (300 baseline, new tests add) · harness shots read at 390 vs the mocks.
