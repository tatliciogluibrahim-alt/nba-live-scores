## First comment (post it yourself, right after submitting)

I built No Noise Scores because every sports app I tried was a feed:
headlines, betting odds and buzzes for teams I don't care about. This
one only knows the teams you follow. Each team gets its own alert level.
Quiet sends the start and the final, usually two pushes a game.

No-Spoilers hides scores everywhere, including push text and the iPhone
lock screen, until you tap a game. The hidden score never even reaches
the lock screen's copy of the game. It's live for the NFL season, on the
App Store and the web.

One detail people here might like: every Tuesday a scheduled job replays
Sunday's real play-by-play through the production push pipeline and
checks that each alert level kept its promise. It caught missed field
goals going out as "big plays" this week.

I built it alone, with AI coding tools doing most of the typing. Happy to
answer anything.
