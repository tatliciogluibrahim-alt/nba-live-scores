**3-post thread**

1. I built a calm sports app. It only follows your teams, and each one
   gets its own alert level. No feed, no ads, no account.
   [Screenshot: `06-following.png`]

2. The design rule I care about most: a hidden score is never in the
   page at all. Not blurred, absent. Screen readers say "score hidden,"
   and the lock screen gets the score only after you tap.
   [Screenshot: `03-nospoilers.png`]

3. Every Tuesday a job replays the weekend's real play-by-play through
   the alert code to check each level kept its promise. This week it
   caught missed field goals going out as "big plays."
