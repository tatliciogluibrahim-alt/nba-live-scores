## Name

No Noise Scores

## Tagline (under 60 characters)

Follow what matters. Skip the rest.

(35 characters. It's the locked tagline, so use it as written.)

## Description (250 to 500 characters)

A calm sports companion for the moments that matter. Follow only your
teams and set how loud each one is: Quiet sends the start and the final,
Companion adds the key beats, Full Details sends everything. No-Spoilers
hides scores in the app, in push text and on the lock screen until you
tap a game. Live for the NFL season on iPhone and the web. No feed, no
ads, no betting.

(About 420 characters.)

## Maker comment

Hi, I'm the one person behind No Noise Scores. I built it because sports
apps kept buzzing about teams I don't follow and spoiling games I was
saving for later. It only knows the teams you pick, and each one gets
its own alert level. No-Spoilers holds back every score, including on
the iPhone lock screen, until you tap. Every Tuesday a scheduled job
replays the weekend's real play-by-play through the alert system to
check that each level sent only what it promises. It's free, with alerts
on your first three follows. I'd love to hear what you'd cut, not what
you'd add.
