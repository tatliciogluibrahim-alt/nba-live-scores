## Before you send

- Do not call No-Spoilers a first. [NoSpoilerz](https://www.nospoilerz.com/), [NoLookSports](https://www.nolooksports.com/guides/best-apps-hide-scores/) and [ScoreCover](https://apps.apple.com/mx/app/scorecover/id6744701173) already sell spoiler-free sports apps.
- The pitch angles say No-Spoilers keeps the score hidden until you tap. Keep the stronger "the lock screen never receives a hidden score" line out until v1.0.5 is live (see `docs/marketing/README.md`).
- Defector appears twice, as Israel Daramola (newsletters) and All Ball (podcasts). Send one, then wait a week before the other.
- Sporticast and the Club Sportico newsletter are the same team. Pitch Feldman once.
